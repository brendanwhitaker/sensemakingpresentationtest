Your "Why"

Slide 1
[ VISUAL ]

A split screen: on one side, a sleek, highly professional digital portfolio; on the other, a tangled, messy web representing the real world.

You are here for an intensive applied learning experience. By using your emerging tech skills to tackle complex real-world issues in Civics & Elections, you will build a portfolio of work that is not just well-coded, but truly remarkable AND deeply meaningful.

Not a Simple Puzzle

Slide 2
[ VISUAL ]

A chaotic, shifting constellation of interconnected dots.

To build that remarkable portfolio, you can't solve simple puzzles. The issues we face are open, complex, dynamic, and networked problem situations. They are messy, lack clear boundaries, and change rapidly.

The Traps to Avoid

Slide 3
[ VISUAL ]

A person trapped in a small cardboard box, and a lone knight facing a dragon.

Resist the urge to jump straight to coding. If you lock onto an immediate solution, you trap yourself in a "self-made box" of obvious ideas, or become an isolated "lone warrior" trying to own the problem without collaboration.

The 9-Step Process

Slide 4
[ VISUAL ]

A list of the 9 steps of Frame Innovation: 1. Archaeology, 2. Paradox, 3. Context, 4. Field, 5. Themes, 6. Frames, 7. Futures, 8. Transformation, 9. Integration.

To solve the unsolvable, we adapt Kees Dorst’s 9-Step Frame Innovation process. Today, our Sprint focuses purely on mapping the problem using Steps 1 through 5.

Not a Staircase, a Cycle

Slide 5
[ VISUAL ]

A swirling, recursive spiral rather than a straight staircase.

Don't be fooled by the numbered list. Frame innovation is "deceptively linear". In reality, it is a recursive cycle where you will constantly zoom in on the details and zoom out to see the big picture.

Solo Sensemaking

Slide 6 (Sprint Phase 1)
[ VISUAL ]

A detective’s evidence board with strings connecting data points.

Step 3 & 4 (Context & Field). Start individually. Use your tech skills as sensemaking instruments. Radically widen the arena to map the broad "field" of all potential players and their power dynamics.

The Mash-Up

Slide 7 (Sprint Phase 2)
[ VISUAL ]

Several overlapping maps merging into one large landscape.

Step 5 (Themes). Merge your individual maps with peers for a "feature harvest." Look for the deep, universal human needs—like trust, isolation, or identity—that are driving the behavior of the players.

The Fork

Slide 8 (Sprint Phase 3)
[ VISUAL ]

A fork in the road leading to a giant question mark.

Step 2 (Paradox). Break away to propose an evidence-backed problem arena built around a core "paradox". You are not pitching a solution; you are pitching a compelling, unresolved mystery.

Curation

Slide 9 (Sprint Phase 4)
[ VISUAL ]

Ideas pinned to a bright office wall.

The community votes, and 4 to 6 seeds become Candidate Pods. If your idea isn't chosen, it's not a "no." Unused ideas become our shared "intellectual capital" saved for future inspiration.

Team Up

Slide 10 (Sprint Phase 5)
[ VISUAL ]

A diverse group of people huddling around a single blueprint.

Join a Candidate Pod. Stop working solo. Co-evolve your understanding of the problem and consolidate your collective evidence into one rich, complex map.

The EOD Pitch

Slide 11 (Today's Deliverable)
[ VISUAL ]

A sleek, high-impact website landing page on a laptop screen.

By the End of the Day, your Candidate Pod will deliver a presentation and a website. Pitch an irresistible opportunity for investigation to the entire upskilling community.

The Ultimate Test

Slide 12 (Deadline: Aug 1)
[ VISUAL ]

A progress bar crossing a red "12+" threshold line.

Your Candidate Pod must use your EOD pitch to recruit 12 or more active members. Only teams that cross this threshold will officially initiate and advance.

The Month of Saturation

Slide 13 (July 26 – Aug 10)
[ VISUAL ]

People out in a city street, observing and taking notes on tablets.

Leave the building. Immerse yourselves in the field to gain firsthand experience and build genuine empathy. Use your tech to test if your themes hold up against real-world behaviors.

Meet the Pods

Slide 14 (Aug 11)
[ VISUAL ]

A bustling, high-energy recruitment fair.

The exclusive showcase for official 12+ person Pods. Present your deep research and real-world empathy. Invite even more upskillers to join your cause for the Hackathon.

The Creative Leap

Slide 15 (Due Aug 15)
[ VISUAL ]

A person looking through a uniquely shaped window frame.

Step 6 (Frames). With your massive Pod aligned, make the creative leap. Invent your Frame—a completely new perspective phrased as: "If the problem situation is approached as if it is... then...".

The Hackathon

Slide 16 (Aug 15)
[ VISUAL ]

A large tree branching off into several fast-growing branches.

Step 7 (Futures). Your 12+ Pod now branches into lean 3-5 person proto-project teams. Use your shared frame to brainstorm rapidly and test completely different tech solutions simultaneously.

Prototyping Futures

Slide 17
[ VISUAL ]

Rapid digital wireframes and lightweight app interfaces.

Don't commit to a single massive build yet. Create lightweight digital prototypes and interactive scenarios to test how your proposed tech solutions will actually play out in the real world.

The Crucible

Slide 18 (Aug 16 - Sept 14)
[ VISUAL ]

A tech team shaking hands with a community organization leader.

Step 8 (Transformation). Critically evaluate feasibility. You must use your prototype to broker an alliance with a specific client or coalition willing to partner with you.

Meet the Projects

Slide 19 (Sept 15)
[ VISUAL ]

A graduation cap sitting on top of a server rack.

Secure your "Problem Owner." The 3-5 person teams that successfully secure a real-world partner graduate to become official Project Teams, locking in their focus for the final build.

Demo Day & Showcase

Slide 20 (Oct 13)
[ VISUAL ]

A fully functioning tech app being used seamlessly in a real-world setting.

Step 9 (Integration). The finish line. You deliver the final, fully functional tech build, beautifully integrated into the everyday context of your problem owner. This is your remarkable and meaningful portfolio.
